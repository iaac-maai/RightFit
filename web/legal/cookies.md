# Cookie Policy

**Version 2026-11-20.**

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains which cookies RightFit online uses, as Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 on information society services (LSSI-CE) and Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC require. A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser and that the browser sends back with each request.

## 2. Only cookies the Service cannot work without

RightFit online uses only cookies that are strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for: keeping you signed in and keeping sign-in secure. The law does not require consent for such cookies, so the Service shows no cookie banner. It uses **no** analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies, and no third-party cookies.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `better-auth.session_token` (on secure connections, `__Secure-better-auth.session_token`) | keeps you signed in; it holds a signed session identifier, not your password | up to 7 days; deleted when you sign out | RightFit online (first party) |
| a short-lived sign-in security cookie | while you sign in with Google, checks that the sign-in started in your own browser | a few minutes; deleted when the sign-in ends | RightFit online (first party) |
| `rf_team` | opens a locked project after you give the team password; it holds a hash of the password, never the password | 7 days | RightFit online (first party) |

The session cookie is marked `HttpOnly`, so scripts on the page cannot read it, and `SameSite=Lax`, so other websites cannot use it to act on your behalf. On secure connections it is also marked `Secure`.

## 3. Google's pages

If you choose to sign in with Google, you are taken to Google's own pages for a moment. The cookies Google uses there are governed by [Google's policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies), not by this one.

## 4. Other storage

The Service does not store information in your browser's local storage or session storage for tracking.

## 5. Your choices

You can delete cookies or block them in your browser's settings at any time. If you block the session cookie, you will not be able to sign in.
