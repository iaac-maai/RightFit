**Version 2026-11-26.** This policy explains how RightFit online handles personal data, as Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the **GDPR**) require. It is written to be read in full; the tables summarise it.

## 2. Two different roles

2.1. **As controller**, we process the data needed to run the Service itself: your account, your sign-in, your place in each project, invitations, the record of the terms you accepted, and the activity log. Sections 3 to 9 describe this processing.

2.2. **As processor**, we host and process the content of each project (material approval forms, attached documents, comments and decisions, and any personal data in them, such as the names and signatures of the people who prepared or signed a document) on behalf of the organisation that opened the project (the **Customer**). The Customer is the controller of that content and decides why it is processed; our [Data Processing Agreement](/data-processing) governs it. On the Customer's instruction, we also keep a record of the products that come back from one of its projects to the next (the product line, the forms that submitted it, the answers they received and a fingerprint of each document) and show it to the people who act for the Customer in its other projects; a document never leaves its project ([Data Processing Agreement](/data-processing), clause 1.4). If you want to exercise your rights over such content, contact the Customer; we will help them answer you.

| Category | What it contains | Where it comes from |

We do not use cookies or other technology to track you, we do not profile you, and we do not process special categories of personal data about you.

## 4. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

**Our legitimate interests** are to provide a secure and accountable Service to the parties of a construction project and to protect it against misuse. We have weighed them against your interests and rights: the data involved is limited to professional contact and usage data, it is visible only to the people who need it, and you can object at any time (section 8).

We do not rely on consent for any of this processing, and we do not send marketing.

## 8. Your rights

8.1. You have the right to: **access** your personal data (Article 15 GDPR); have it **corrected** (Article 16); have it **erased** (Article 17); **restrict** its processing (Article 18); receive it in a structured, machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller (**portability**, Article 20); and **object** at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21).

8.2. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from the Service. For any other request, write to {{operator.email}}. We answer within one month, which may be extended by two further months for complex requests, as Article 12(3) GDPR allows. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

8.3. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement (Article 77 GDPR). In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)); in Romania, the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal ([dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro)).

## 9. Do you have to give us the data?

Your name and email address are needed to create an account and to take part in projects; without them we cannot provide the Service. Giving your company is optional.

## 10. Automated decisions

We do not take decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling, within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. The drafts RightFit produces concern documents and products, not people, and a person always decides on them.

## 11. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (Article 32 GDPR), including: encryption of all traffic (TLS); passwords stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes; invitation links stored only as hashes; tokens received from Google stored encrypted; access to each project checked on our servers for every request; a limit on repeated sign-in attempts; Management access limited to named persons signed in with a Google-verified account, with every change logged; and no customer content in our public source code repository. Annex II of the [Data Processing Agreement](/data-processing) describes these measures in more detail.

## 12. Children

The Service is for professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children.
