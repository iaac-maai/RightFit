**Version 2026-11-20.** This agreement meets Article 28(3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the **GDPR**).

## Parties

This Data Processing Agreement (the **Agreement**) is entered into between:

- the organisation on whose behalf a consultant opens a project in RightFit online (the **Customer**, the controller), represented by that consultant, who confirms their authority to bind it; and
- {{operator.name}}, {{operator.address}}, tax number {{operator.taxId}} (the **Provider**, the processor).

The consultant accepts this Agreement on the Customer's behalf when opening a project. The Service records the version accepted, by whom and when. This Agreement supplements the [Terms of Service](/terms); words defined there have the same meaning here.

## 1. Subject matter and duration

1.1. The Provider processes personal data contained in Customer Content (the **Customer Personal Data**) on behalf of the Customer, solely to provide the Service for the Customer's project.

1.2. This Agreement applies for as long as the Provider processes Customer Personal Data, and ends when that data has been deleted under clause 10.

1.3. Annex I describes the processing: its nature and purpose, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects.

## 2. The Customer's instructions

2.1. The Provider processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including as to transfers to third countries, unless EU or Member State law requires otherwise; in that case the Provider informs the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits it on important grounds of public interest (Article 28(3)(a) GDPR).

2.2. This Agreement, the Terms of Service, and the Customer's and its Members' use of the Service's features constitute the Customer's complete instructions. Further instructions must be in writing and consistent with the Service.

2.3. The Provider informs the Customer immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other data protection law.

2.4. The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of the processing it instructs, including having a legal basis for putting Customer Personal Data into the Service, informing the data subjects, and ensuring that Members who upload content are entitled to share it with the project.

## 3. Confidentiality

The Provider ensures that every person it authorises to process Customer Personal Data has committed to confidentiality or is under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality (Article 28(3)(b) GDPR), and accesses it only as far as needed to provide, support and secure the Service.

## 4. Security

4.1. The Provider implements the technical and organisational measures described in Annex II, which ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as Article 32 GDPR requires.

4.2. The Provider may update those measures, provided that the overall level of security is not reduced.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1. The Customer gives the Provider general written authorisation to engage the sub-processors listed in Annex III.

5.2. The Provider informs the Customer, through the Service or by email to the consultants of the project, at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Customer may close the project and have its content deleted under clause 10, as its sole remedy.

5.3. The Provider imposes on each sub-processor, by contract, data protection obligations that offer at least the same protection as this Agreement, in particular sufficient guarantees of appropriate technical and organisational measures. The Provider remains fully liable to the Customer for the performance of each sub-processor's obligations (Article 28(4) GDPR).

## 6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The Provider transfers Customer Personal Data to a country outside the European Economic Area only where a safeguard under Chapter V GDPR applies: an adequacy decision, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients, or the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, together with any supplementary measures needed. Annex III states the safeguard for each sub-processor.

## 7. Assistance to the Customer

7.1. **Data subjects' rights.** Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Provider assists the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III GDPR (Article 28(3)(e)). If a data subject contacts the Provider directly about Customer Personal Data, the Provider refers them to the Customer and informs the Customer without undue delay.

7.2. **Other obligations.** The Provider assists the Customer in ensuring compliance with Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, notification of breaches, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation), taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it (Article 28(3)(f)).

## 8. Personal data breaches

8.1. The Provider notifies the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data (Article 33(2) GDPR).

8.2. The notification describes, as far as then known: the nature of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned; the likely consequences; the measures taken or proposed to address it and to mitigate its possible adverse effects; and a contact point. Information not yet available is provided as it becomes available.

8.3. The Provider takes reasonable measures to contain the breach and to prevent it from recurring, and documents every breach.

## 9. Information and audits

9.1. The Provider makes available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR, and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor it mandates (Article 28(3)(h)).

9.2. The Customer gives at least 30 days' notice of an audit, which takes place during business hours, no more than once a year unless a breach or an authority requires otherwise, at the Customer's own cost, and under a duty of confidentiality. Audits of sub-processors are carried out by relying on their certifications and reports where these are sufficient.

## 10. Deletion or return at the end

10.1. When a project is deleted, or when the Service ends, the Provider deletes the Customer Personal Data of that project, unless EU or Member State law requires it to be stored (Article 28(3)(g) GDPR). Before then, and on request made at least 15 days before deletion, the Provider gives the Customer a copy of the project's content in a common format.

10.2. Copies in the hosting provider's backups are erased within 30 days after deletion from the live database.

## 11. Liability

Each party's liability under this Agreement is subject to Article 82 GDPR and to the limitations in the Terms of Service, except where those limitations are not permitted by law.

## 12. Precedence, changes and law

12.1. In case of conflict, this Agreement prevails over the Terms of Service as regards the processing of personal data.

12.2. The Provider may change this Agreement to reflect changes in law or in the Service. A new version is accepted by a consultant of the project; if it is not accepted, the version last accepted continues to apply, and either party may close the project.

12.3. This Agreement is governed by Spanish law, and the courts of Barcelona have jurisdiction, without prejudice to the powers of supervisory authorities.

## Annex II: Technical and organisational measures

**Access control**

- every request is authenticated on the server; access to a project is checked for each request against the user's membership and role, and a user outside a project receives "not found";
- each Member sees only what their role allows; subcontractors are isolated from each other's branches;
- Management access is limited to persons named by the Provider, signed in with a Google-verified account, and every change they make is written to an activity log kept for 12 months.

**Authentication**

- passwords of at least 10 characters, stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes;
- sign-in with Google through OAuth 2.0, with the tokens received from Google stored encrypted;
- sessions of at most 7 days, held in an HttpOnly, SameSite cookie, and revocable by Management;
- limits on repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts.

**Invitations**

- single-use links built from 32 random bytes, valid for 14 days, bound to one email address; only a hash of the link is stored.

**Transmission and storage**

- all traffic encrypted with TLS; data stored by the hosting provider, which encrypts it at rest;
- secrets (signing keys, client secrets) held as platform secrets, never in source code;
- no customer content or personal data in the public source code repository.

**Integrity and resilience**

- the hosting provider's managed database with point-in-time recovery; protection against denial-of-service attacks at the network edge;
- requests from other websites' pages that would change data are refused.

**Organisation**

- the principles of data minimisation and least privilege in design;
- a written procedure for personal data breaches;
- review of these measures whenever the Service changes significantly.
