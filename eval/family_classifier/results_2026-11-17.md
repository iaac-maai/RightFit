# Family classifier: first version, 17 Nov 2026

Check D1 asks for documents by product family (`data/maf/family_documents.csv`). Today only the AI reader sets the family, for P01 and P02. This model names the family from the product row of the form, so the unread packages of the second batch could get their document list without being read.

## Data

- The product rows of the forms of P03 to P51: 59 rows from 48 forms (`model/family_rows.py`). P18 has no form (its archive is incomplete). Most forms hold one row; the two revisions of the luminaires MAF hold six each.
- Labels: Luca picked the family of every row on 17 Nov 2026, on a page with nothing filled in beforehand, from the 13 families of check D1 plus other. One row he marked unsure is left out.
- Locked rows: every package of the chatbot test set (`eval/chatbot_baseline/test_set.csv`), so RightFit never trains on a package it is compared on, and every MAF joined to one of them: revisions of one MAF, MAFs that share a file, and MAFs whose rows share a product name or a model. No random share was added (Luca's decision): the chatbot set is already a random draw by MAF. Training: 21 rows from 20 MAF groups. Locked: 37 rows from 21 MAF groups.

## The two methods

- Family words (non-AI, `family_words` in `model/family_classifier.py`): each family's name in Romanian and English, its usual synonyms and its standard number, searched in the row's name. When the words of two families are there, the one named first wins; a cable's name must start with the word.
- Our model: letter groups of 2 to 4 inside words (TF-IDF) and a logistic regression, trained from scratch on the 21 training rows. Three variants were compared by cross-validation on the training rows only; the one that reads the name, the brand and the model was kept.

## Cross-validation on the training rows

Each MAF group is left out in turn. The locked rows were not used.

| | Rows right | Rows of a family right |
|---|---|---|
| Family words | 20 of 21 | 8 of 9 |
| Model, name only, groups of 2 to 4 | 16 of 21 | 5 of 9 |
| Model, name, brand and model, groups of 2 to 4 (kept) | 17 of 21 | 5 of 9 |
| Model, name only, groups of 3 to 5 | 16 of 21 | 5 of 9 |

The model cannot get a family that has a single training row: when that row is left out, it has seen no example of it.

## Score on the locked rows

Scored once, on 17 Nov 2026.

| | Rows right | Rows of a family right |
|---|---|---|
| Family words (non-AI) | 35 of 37 | 5 of 5 |
| Our model | 28 of 37 | 1 of 5 |

- The model found the loudspeaker and called the two insulation rows, the radiator and the fire damper other: it had no training row of these families.
- It called five rows of other equipment of the same voice alarm system loudspeakers: from the two training loudspeakers it learned the manufacturer's name and "EN 54", not what a loudspeaker is.
- The family words found every family row. Their two mistakes are rows Luca labelled other whose names say EN-54: the microphone console of the voice evacuation system (the words said voice_alarm_control) and its 24 V backup supply (the words said fire_alarm_power_supply). See the limits.

Run with `python -m model.family_rows`, then `python -m model.family_classifier` (cross-validation, saves the model) and `python -m model.family_classifier score`.

## Limits

- One project, 59 rows, most forms with one row. 21 training rows cannot teach 13 families: 7 have no training row, 4 more have one. The result shows what 21 rows can do, not what this kind of model can do with enough rows.
- 32 of the 37 locked rows are other, so "rows right" mostly counts other; the family rows are only 5. With this few rows no confidence interval is worth giving.
- The family words were written by the person who prepared the rows, after seeing the row texts but not the labels or any output. That can favour them.
- Four locked rows whose names say EN-54 are labelled other: the system manager of the voice evacuation system (two MAFs with the same row), its microphone console and its 24 V backup supply. Under the family table they could be voice_alarm_control (EN 54-16) and fire_alarm_power_supply (EN 54-4), which ask for a declaration of performance. They are scored as labelled. Which family they belong to is still an open engineering question for check D1.
- A row that holds two products (a radiator with its valves; an outdoor unit with its DX kit) carries the one family Luca picked.
- Shared files were found by file size, not content, because iCloud has moved most package files off the laptop. Equal files always have equal sizes, so this can only join more MAFs, never fewer.
- Labelling showed Luca one row of each chatbot test package before his timed reviews; the inventory already names each product.
