# Page classifier: first run, 29 Oct 2026

The question: what kind of document is this PDF page (declaration, data sheet, certificate, manual, other)? A reviewer needs it to know what a package really contains, and to find declarations hidden inside manuals.

## What we compared
- **Non-AI baseline**: keywords a reviewer would search for, in six languages (`model/page_classifier.py`, `keyword_baseline`).
- **Our model**: trained from scratch, with no pretrained weights. Letter groups of 3 to 5 characters and word pairs (TF-IDF), then a logistic regression. It learns only from our training pages.

## Data
- Training: 33 public PDFs from official manufacturer sites (Grundfos, Wilo, Danfoss, Panasonic, IMI, Hisense, Daikin, Heat Pump KEYMARK, Eurovent), where robots.txt allows it; links and hashes in `training_sources.csv`, copies only on the laptop. 673 labelled pages (`training_labels.csv`); at most 40 pages of one class per document, so 443 pages are used. Two scanned declarations have no text and are left out.
- Locked test, never used for training or tuning (`test_labels.csv`):
  - Set A: the 15 PDFs of the two real packages, 111 pages; 4 are scans without text that neither method can read yet;
  - Set B: two public Grundfos manuals with declarations inside, 47 pages.

## Results

| | Set A, real packages | Set B, manuals with hidden declarations |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline: pages right | 27 % | 45 % |
| Our model: pages right | 25 % | 87 % |
| Baseline: declaration pages found | 30 % of 23 | 100 % of 8 |
| Our model: declaration pages found | 57 % of 23 | 88 % of 8 |
| Baseline: data sheet pages found | 20 % of 81 | — |
| Our model: data sheet pages found | 15 % of 81 | — |
| Baseline: manual pages found | — | 14 % of 29 |
| Our model: manual pages found | — | 86 % of 29 |

In cross-validation on the training documents (each document left out in turn), the model gets 75 % of pages right, but only 6 % of the data sheet pages.

## What this says
- Our model beats the baseline clearly on manuals, and finds almost twice as many declaration pages in the real packages: it recognises the continuation pages of long declarations (model lists, translations), which carry no keyword.
- It fails on data sheets: almost all its training data sheets come from one Grundfos data booklet in English, and none look like the Romanian Grundfos Product Center sheets, the AHU selection printout or the DX reports of the packages. Changing the model (fewer manual pages, number and unit features) did not help in cross-validation, so the fix is data, not the model.
- The baseline is also fooled by documents that mention a declaration without being one: a Danfoss data notice and three reference pages of a Danfoss manual.

## Limits
- The training labels were drafted by the AI reader from each document and from the declaration sections it found. Luca confirms a 104-page sample before the next run.
- The test pages were labelled the same way and need Luca's confirmation too.
- Whether labels drafted by a commercial AI assistant may train our model is still to verify against its terms; once Luca confirms them they are his labels.
- The test sets were scored once with this model. The model was not changed after seeing them.
