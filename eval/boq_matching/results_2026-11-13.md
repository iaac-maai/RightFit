# Finding a product's BOQ line: a study, 13 Nov 2026

How should RightFit find the line of the project's BOQ that a product answers? Until now it matched the duty (Q and H) written in the line, which only pumps have. This study compares four ways on the same cases, before anything is built.

## Cases

24 products from the forms of the project of P01 and P02, whose BOQ is on the laptop: P01, P02 and the second batch packages outside the frozen test set (`eval/chatbot_baseline/test_set.csv`), which were not used. Most forms name the BOQ position (for example `E02.ICS01.01`, or `T03 pozitia 2 si 4`), which gives the right line. For P01 the form names only the sheet; its four lines were found by duty and checked by hand on 12 Nov 2026. Where a form names several positions for one product (sizes of one valve, two cables), any of them counts as right.

## Results

| Method | Right, of 24 |
|---|---|
| Current rule: duty, or position plus family words | 4 |
| The position the form names, when it names exactly one | 15 |
| Text similarity over the whole BOQ | 15 |
| Text similarity inside the sheet the form names | 16 |
| The positions the form names, then text similarity among them (or in the sheet when there is no position) | 24 |

Text similarity: TF-IDF on character n-grams (3 to 5) and cosine similarity, between the product's name, brand and model and each BOQ line (scikit-learn). All methods together take 0.1 s for the 24 cases; none calls an AI model.

## What it shows

- The current rule had a solution for pumps only: 4 of 24.
- The strongest signal is the position the form already names. Text alone confuses close products (indoor and outdoor cameras, a fire panel and its devices).
- Positions first, then text to choose among them, found all 24. Strictly, one choice is doubtful: for P19 it picked the 2x2x0.8 cable where the form says 1x2x1.5; character n-grams read sizes badly.

## Limits

- One project and one BOQ; the second project's BOQs have no forms to test against.
- 24 cases; with several positions on one form the check is lenient.
- The method was designed after seeing these cases; the frozen test set will show whether it holds.

## Built, same day

`ingest/boq.py` `find_line` now works this way: the positions the form names; several, then the closest text; no position, the duty in the form's sheet; else the closest text, marked as matched by text only. The text compared is the form's own row for the product (its name, brand and model as written on the form), because the readings may hold a translated name. Run on the 24 cases it finds 24; P01 keeps its four lines (by duty) and P02 finds the AHU at T03 row 17 and the outdoor units at row 19.
