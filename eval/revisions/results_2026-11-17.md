# What changed between two revisions: first run, 17 Nov 2026

`ingest/revisions.py` pairs each attachment of a new revision of a MAF with the one of the previous revision that it continues (spec section 6, MAF-26). No AI: file digests, then text similarity (TF-IDF on groups of 3 to 5 characters), or the look of the first pages for a scan.

## Data

One pair: P10 (form IV 004, revision 3, 7 attachments) to P02 (revision 4, 9 attachments). It is the only pair of revisions outside the frozen chatbot test set (`eval/chatbot_baseline/test_set.csv`); P12 to P13 and P14 to P15 are in it, so they were not opened. The thresholds were set on this pair, so it shows the method working, not how well it works on unseen revisions.

## Result

| Status | Attachments |
|---|---|
| unchanged | 5: the Eurovent certificate, the TÜV certificate and the Hisense declaration under the same names; the "All Products" declaration and the AHU kit sheet renamed |
| changed | 1: the AHU data sheet, text similarity 0.91; none of its 30 pages kept exactly the same text |
| new | 3: the two DX selection reports and the outdoor units' range sheet |
| removed | 1: the previous outdoor unit sheet |

- The AHU kit sheet of revision 4, "AHU KIT HZX-6BEJ.pdf", is byte for byte the file "AHU KIT HZX-20BEJ.pdf" of revision 3: only its name changed. A reviewer comparing names would read it as a new document.
- Matching by file name alone pairs 3 of the 6 attachments that the comparison pairs.
- Between different documents the similarity stayed at or below 0.29 by text and 0.42 by look, against thresholds of 0.5 and 0.8. The two scans of outdoor units (0.42) come back as one removed and one new, not as one changed.
- The comparison takes about 2.6 s on this pair, so the review page shows it after the rest of the page.

## Limits

- One pair, from one project. The pairs were not checked by Luca.
- Both scans that continue were identical files, caught by their digest, so no real pair of scans backs the look threshold; the unit tests use drawn pages.
- Two different documents with near identical text, such as two declarations from one template, can be paired as one changed document; the reviewer opens both files.
- Thumbnails compare pages where they sit; a page scanned with a shift or a rotation looks different. A trained page encoder, as in the hackathon, is the upgrade if that happens on real scans.

Run with `python3 -m ingest.revisions P02` (finds P10 by the form number) or `python3 -m ingest.revisions P10 P02`.
